About Me

Hello!

I'm Shawn, a Software Developer.

On this website, I showcase my projects, write about their underlying technologies, share my hobbies, and anything else that I want to put out into the world.

Everything is written in my own words and opinions. It serves as a personal space for self-reflection and retrospection, for sharing what I have learned and am currently learning, and connecting with the people reading it. I hope to inspire others to create their own corner of the web and push back against the constant noise of the modern internet and social media. As such, there will be no Ads, AI-generated content, or analytics found here.

I got into software development because I enjoy tinkering and diving into how and why things work. I have always had broad interests spanning from computer science and web development to ecology and agriculture. More specifically, how software and data-driven approaches can support agriculture, while benefiting farms and ecosystems through efficiency and sustainability.

Welcome to my corner of the web!
