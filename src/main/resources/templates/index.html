About This Site

On this website, I showcase my projects, write about their underlying technologies, share my hobbies, and anything else that I want to put out into the world.

Everything is written in my own words and opinions. It serves as a personal space for self-reflection and retrospection, for sharing what I have learned and am currently learning, and connecting with the people reading it. I hope to inspire others to create their own corner of the web and push back against the constant noise of the modern internet.
