About Me

I'm Shawn, a Software Developer. On this website, I showcase my projects, write about their underlying technologies, share my hobbies, and anything else I want to put out into the world.

I enjoy tinkering and diving into how and why things work. I have broad interests in various systems, spanning from computer systems to car engines to biology and ecosystems. I am currently a stay-at-home father of two kids and a dog, a homeschool teacher, and a husband. Looking for a remote (or local to the northern Illinois / southern Wisconsin area) opportunity to work in full-stack software development, contributing to meaningful systems for a responsible company.

Everything here is written with my own words and opinions. You will never see a single ad, AI-generated content, or tracking here. This site is my personal space for self-reflection, sharing what I have learned and what I am currently learning, and connecting with the readers of that content. It is also my attempt to inspire others to create their own personal space and participate in the fight against the noise and enshittification of our internet.
